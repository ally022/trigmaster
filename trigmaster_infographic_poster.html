About This App

What is TrigMaster?

TrigMaster is an all-in-one Precalculus web app built to make right-angle trigonometry easy. Instead of just giving you a final number, the app walks you through how to label sides (o, a, h), pick SOH-CAH-TOA, and solve step by step—with 4 built-in tabs: Solver, Elevation & Depression, Learn, and Practice.

How it prevents mistakes: The app automatically warns you if RAD mode is on and catches common multiply-vs-divide traps!

The rules it uses

SOH-CAH-TOA Ratios

Match the 2 sides you have (o, a, or h) to the right trig ratio:

sin(θ) = oh   cos(θ) = ah
tan(θ) = oa

x on Top vs. x on Bottom

Put 1 underneath and cross-multiply to isolate x:

sin(θ)1 = ox → x = osin(θ)
Top → Multiply • Bottom → Divide

Inverse Trig (Find Angle θ)

Use inverse functions to "undo" sin, cos, or tan:

sin(θ) = oh → θ = sin-1(oh)

Calculator & Triangle Checks

Always verify DEG mode and hypotenuse length:

Hypotenuse (h) must be the longest side, and calculator must be in DEG (°).

Worked example

Find x when θ = 35° and o = 12
Steps
1
Label sides: Across from 35° is o = 12; across from 90° is h = x.
2
Choose SOH: sin(35°) = 12x
3
Put 1 underneath & cross-multiply:
sin(35°)1 = 12x
4
Divide & cancel:
x · sin(35°)sin(35°) = 12sin(35°)
5
Calculate (DEG): x ≈ 20.9 (20.9 > 12 ✓)
Triangle
Solution:
x = 12sin(35°) ≈ 20.9
